## Priority logic

- **A** — Clear public contact path plus strong reach or timing: national orgs and their wellness/sports/women channels, large-metro associations, targets with a fitness or health program history, the Dec 18 TAGB ladies night (day before the taster), Telugu Moms Network, the program coach’s Instagram (cross-promo), and the strongest US Telugu creator found.
- **B** — Solid local associations, temples (bulletin/newsletter usually needs board or manager approval), Meetup groups, and mid-size US Telugu creators. Worth contacting in the same week, after the first A-list notes go out.
- **C** — Weaker or incomplete contact paths (missing email, login-walled size, possible promo restrictions, or not Telugu-specific). Park these until A/B replies are in.

WhatsApp groups are generally private. This batch does not invent join links. If an association later advertises a public WhatsApp entry path, add it to the CSV then.

Coach Instagram is a cross-promo asset, not just a bio link: [Anish Jakkula](https://www.instagram.com/anish_jakkula) (`@anish_jakkula`). In public copy, describe 11 years of experience and the specialties below as specialties, not certifications: female fitness; special populations (beginners, moms, desk-job folks); fat loss/body recomp; strength & conditioning; functional training; personalized coaching.
