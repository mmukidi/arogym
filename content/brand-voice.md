# Brand Voice Guide (one page) — AROGYM

**Name:** AROGYM (say "aa-ro-gym"), from *arogyam* (health) + gym. Always all caps in logos and headlines; "Arogym" is fine in running text and hashtags (#AROGYM, #Arogym30).
**Tagline options:** "Mana arogyam, mana gym." · "Health, the Telugu way." · "30 rojulu. Mana community. Live."

**Who we talk to:** US Telugu IT professionals (25–45), moms juggling work + kids, families. Busy, desk-bound, missing home food, a little guilty about skipped workouts.

**Our personality:** The encouraging *akka/anna* from home who happens to be a trainer. Warm, practical, a little funny, never preachy.

## Voice in 4 words
1. **Warm** — like a call from home. "Emi parledu, start cheddam."
2. **Practical** — 30 minutes, at home, after work. No gym, no fancy gear.
3. **Proud** — our food, our language, our people. Pesarattu is not the enemy.
4. **Honest** — real progress, no miracle claims.

## Language
- **Tenglish is the default.** English sentence spine + Telugu flavor words: *chala, parledu, ready na?, cheddam, mana, ammo, bagundi.*
- Use full Telugu script only in hooks/titles where it pops (e.g. "మన 30 రోజులు").
- Short sentences. One idea per line in captions.
- Times are always **ET** ("8 PM ET, Mon–Fri").

## We say / We don't say
| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "Stronger, more energy, better sleep" | "Lose 10 kg in 30 days" |
| "Eat our food, smarter portions" | "Quit rice forever" |
| "Show up, that's the win" | "No excuses!" / shaming |
| "Your trainer from Hyderabad, live" | "Celebrity / guaranteed results" |
| "Mana community" | Medical claims, "cure", "detox" |

## Hooks pattern (Reels)
Relatable pain → Telugu twist → tiny promise. e.g.
- "Office lo 9 hours kurchunnava? 8 PM ki 30 min ivvu. AROGYM 30-day challenge."
- "Amma cheppindi 'exercise cheyyi'. Ippudu live lo cheddam."
- "Biryani vadalakkarledu. Portion matrame marchu."

## Visual/tone cues
Bright, homely, real people (no stock fitness models). Festive touches (Sankranti falls mid-challenge: use it). Emojis light: 💪🔥🙏 max 2 per caption.

## Guardrails
- Every diet/health line is a draft until the trainer approves.
- Always point to the health form/PAR-Q; "check with your doctor" on any health-condition content.
- No before/after body-shaming; testimonials only with written consent.
