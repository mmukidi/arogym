# Website content, design system and editorial strategy

## Primary message
**AROGYM — Your Food. Your Fitness. Your Way.**  
Hero headline option: **Fitness That Fits Your Life.**  
Subhead: “Personalized coaching that respects the food you love, the time you have, your training environment and your goals.”

### CTAs
- **Primary:** “Book a FREE 30-Min Consultation” → Anish's scheduling page.
- **Secondary:** “Chat on WhatsApp” → business-owned WhatsApp.
- **Exploration:** “Find My Fitness Plan” → optional short non-medical quiz.
Never imply the CTA is functional until configured.

## Brand personality
Premium athletic yet approachable; inclusive of men, women, beginners, WFH professionals, older adults and athletes. Indian cultural connection through real food, languages and coach experience, not stereotypes.

## Provisional visual direction (not yet approved)
- Hybrid dark hero + bright warm lifestyle sections.
- Dark forest `#17231D`, warm ivory `#F4F2EA`, fresh lime `#C7F47A`, charcoal `#202820`; test contrast and accessible alternatives.
- Strong modern typography, generous negative space, subtle motion with reduced-motion support.
- Real coach photography, authentic regional Indian meals, real home/gym/sports settings. Avoid exclusively muscular imagery.

## Navigation
Home | Find Your Plan | Programs | Food & Nutrition | Sports Performance | Our Coaches | Resources | [Free Consultation]  
Persistent WhatsApp link; footer with contact, policies, countries served and languages.

## Homepage narrative order
1. Hero + direct booking / WhatsApp.
2. Why AROGYM: personalized across food, schedule, abilities, preferences and goals.
3. Goals cards: fat loss, muscle, strength, sports conditioning, active living.
4. “How we tailor your plan”: goals, food, time/equipment, coach adjustment.
5. Indian food: Telugu launch, expansion to other cuisines as real content becomes available.
6. Programs and what coaching includes.
7. 4-step journey: discover → consult → personalize → progress.
8. Meet Anish, with verified qualifications.
9. Real client testimonials **only if available and consented**.
10. Resource content: evidence-based food, exercise, supplement explainers.
11. FAQs + fixed 30-minute CTA + WhatsApp.

## Individual pages
- **Programs**: Body Transformation; Home & Busy Life; Sports Performance; Strength & Active Living; Women's Fitness; Advanced Physique.
- **Food & Nutrition**: regional cuisines, veg/nonveg/vegan preferences, practical portion literacy, protein sources, easy meal prep and grocery swaps.
- **Sports Performance**: strength and conditioning, training specificity, readiness screening and scope limitations.
- **Coaches**: Anish's intro, credentials *after verification*, training approach, specialities, book call.
- **Resources**: exercise technique, work-from-home movement, Indian recipes, training consistency, supplement evidence and safety.
- **FAQ**: home vs gym; time commitment; how meal customization works; languages; booking; session format; refunds; whether supplements are required (no).
- **Policies**: Privacy, Terms, Cookies as applicable, Coaching/medical disclaimer, Accessibility, Contact.

## Editorial content pillars
1. Indian food explained: Telugu/Telangana/Andhra launch, other cuisines later.
2. Strength and body recomposition without extreme claims.
3. 20–45 minute routines for WFH and busy schedules.
4. Women's fitness and healthy aging, where coaches are suitably qualified.
5. Sports conditioning for soccer/cricket/running and others as expertise allows.
6. Supplements: protein, creatine, caffeine, electrolytes, risks and third-party testing.
7. Coach credibility: demonstrations, responsible testimonials, coaching process.

## SEO/localization
Country-neutral main brand; locale routes e.g. `/en/`, `/te/` when translations approved; `hreflang` and canonical tags; accessible alt text; image compression; recipe and organization schema only where valid. Never claim support for languages/countries before service is ready.

## Editorial governance
Every article has author, reviewer where appropriate, publication/review date, source notes and translation status. No invented endorsements, fabricated metrics, medical claims or testimonials.
