# Vision, positioning and strategy

## Mission
Make effective fitness coaching practical and culturally familiar, adapting movement, nutrition guidance and accountability to each individual's lifestyle, rather than demanding people abandon their culture, work schedule or favorite foods.

## Long-term vision
One global brand supporting multilingual coaches, regional Indian cuisine libraries, strength and conditioning, healthy habits, sport-specific performance, and personalized digital experiences.

## Initial wedge
- Telugu-speaking adults in India and the USA/diaspora.
- Founding coach: Anish Jakkula; experience and capabilities reported by founder, certifications not yet independently verified.
- Premium individual online coaching and optional scalable group offerings, based on verified capacity.
- Discovery and sales via social content, website, **30-minute free consultation**, and WhatsApp Business.

## Customer segments
1. Busy professionals / work-from-home: short effective sessions, minimal equipment, realistic meal preparation.
2. Body composition and strength: sustainable fat loss, muscle development, progression and accountability.
3. Women seeking personalized coaching: coach suitability, goals, preferences, and safe adaptation.
4. Recreational athletes: sport-specific strength, conditioning and movement competence; no diagnosis or injury treatment.
5. Beginners and active-aging adults: gradual progression, appropriate accommodations and referral when needed.
6. Advanced physique clients: hypertrophy and bodybuilding prep, contingent on coach qualifications and defined service scope.

## Positioning
**AROGYM — Your Food. Your Fitness. Your Way.**  
Fitness that fits real lives: personalized workouts, culturally relevant food education, flexible scheduling, expert-led coaching.

### Differentiation pillars
- Real human coach + regular accountability.
- Food culture supported, not stigmatized: Telugu initially, other cuisines later.
- Training preference: home, gym, hybrid, variable schedules.
- Goal specificity: strength, fat loss, muscle, sport, sustainable daily habits.
- Fitness for different abilities with safe screening/referral boundaries.
- International service with local languages, time zones and payment methods as supported.

## Business model hypothesis (not validated)
- First: paid 12-week personalized coaching after a free 30-minute consultation.
- Optional: group coaching and more intensive 1:1 sessions at distinct pricing.
- Later: multi-coach offerings and software-supported recurring memberships.
- Do not promise results, revenue, or conversion rates without evidence.

## North-star and operating metrics
- North star: **active paying clients receiving and completing coaching check-ins**.
- Website: qualified enquiry rate; consultation-booking conversion; source-to-consultation conversion.
- Sales: consultation attendance; paid conversion; customer acquisition cost; refunds.
- Retention: 30/90-day engagement, renewals, client-reported progress and satisfaction.
- Delivery: coach utilization, response times, missed sessions, safeguarding escalations.
- Content: saves, shares, website visits, **qualified leads**, not just Reel views.

## Initial pilot success criteria (targets, not forecasts)
- 30–45 qualified consultations, 10–15 paying founding clients, and 20+ relevant education videos in first 90 days.
- Measure actual CAC, conversion, coach hours per client, support load and client feedback before scaling ads.

## Principles
- Trust over transformation hype.
- Accessible, mobile-first and inclusive.
- Privacy by design and least data collected.
- Brand and platform independent of any single coach, state or language.
- Measure demand before adding expensive features.
