# Security, privacy, safety and compliance plan

**Not legal or clinical advice.** Obtain qualified reviews for relevant India/US states and every new service market.

## Risk classifications
- **Public:** page content, approved trainer bio, consented imagery.
- **Business confidential:** contracts, pricing strategies, vendor keys, lead lists.
- **Personal:** name, email, telephone, contact preferences.
- **Potentially sensitive:** injury disclosures, symptoms, measurements, progress photos, nutrition history, health conditions.
Never store personal or sensitive information in GitHub Pages, public code, client-side local storage by default, or analytics event parameters.

## Minimum safeguards before launch
- Secure HTTPS and approved domain ownership; restricted GitHub collaborators, branch rules, MFA.
- Secrets only in provider-managed secrets; never in repository or client JavaScript.
- Booking/CRM vendor DPA, permissions and retention review; strong access controls.
- Explicit privacy notice, service terms, cancellation/rescheduling terms and marketing consent.
- Separate WhatsApp customer-initiated enquiries from promotional messages; appropriate opt-in for outbound marketing.
- Visible contact channel and procedure for data access/deletion requests.
- Restricted access to customer information; documented incident escalation.
- Images/testimonials used only with copyright, publicity and participant permissions.
- Supplement and nutrition guidance medically reviewed when necessary; avoid unsubstantiated marketing claims.

## Scope-of-practice guardrails
- Fitness coaching is not physical therapy, diagnosis, medical rehabilitation or treatment.
- Injury-related requests: screen for safe participation, obtain clearance or refer as clinically appropriate.
- Nutrition: general education and food planning within verified qualifications; individualized therapeutic diet plans require appropriately qualified professionals.
- Supplements: evidence-based informational articles; no high-risk product sales or automatic dose recommendations for medical conditions.
- Sports performance: conditioning and training, not injury treatment or medical performance guarantees.
- Separate policies for minors; default initial service audience adults until safeguarding/consent procedures are reviewed.

## Regulatory/legal workstreams (verify applicability)
- USA: FTC advertising/testimonials and health claim substantiation; state-specific coaching/nutrition scopes; consumer/privacy and tax rules.
- India: consumer/advertising and privacy requirements, trainer contracting, cross-border service/payment rules.
- Global: applicable privacy regulations (e.g. GDPR where serving eligible audiences), tax, currency and consumer cancellation rights.
- Accessibility: aim for WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Business formation, coach independent-contractor vs employee status, insurance, cross-border tax handling, liability waiver/limitations and dispute terms need professional review.

## Practical launch policies
- No fake before/after photos, fabricated certification badges or outcome guarantees.
- Clear “Free 30-minute consultation” scope and no-obligation language.
- Minimize consultation booking intake; sensitive screening through secure approved channel.
- GDPR-style purpose limitation as default design practice regardless of geography.
- No automated WhatsApp outbound messages without verified provider policies/opt-in.
- Build deletion, breach response, permissions review and backup/restore drills before storing sensitive member data.

## Security release gate
Privacy notice approved; authorized coach credentials/rights verified; all links use HTTPS; security headers evaluated; forms tested for spam/data leakage; tracking consent functioning; incident owner named; vendor list and DPA status recorded.
